What this is

trade-away is an experiment in building a trading bot that has to prove itself before it touches real money. Four bots each follow one well-known trading method and trade side by side, so we can see which method actually works. Later, an AI agent joins the race. It only goes further if it beats both these simple bots and plain buying and holding the S&P 500.

Everything here is paper trading: real market prices, simulated money. The account started with $100,000, split into $25,000 for each bot. No real money is involved.

The bots

Trend

Rides a trend once it's established. Prices that have been rising tend to keep rising for a while.

Buys
when the 20-day average price crosses above the 50-day average
Sells
when it crosses back below
Trades
US stocks, ETFs, Bitcoin and Ether

Dip buying

Buys a sharp short drop in something that's still in a long uptrend, betting it bounces back within days.

Buys
when RSI(2), a measure of how hard it fell over 2 days, is under 10 and the price is above its 200-day average
Sells
when the price closes back above its 5-day average
Trades
US stocks, ETFs, Bitcoin and Ether

Breakout

The "Turtle" method from the 1980s: buy when the price breaks out to a new high, on the idea that a big move is starting.

Buys
when the price closes above its highest point of the last 20 days
Sells
when it closes below its lowest point of the last 10 days
Trades
US stocks, ETFs, Bitcoin and Ether

Momentum

Owns the recent winners. Stocks that beat the market over the past several months tend to keep doing so.

Buys
the 10 strongest of the most traded US stocks over the last 6 months (leaving out the latest month), if they're above their 200-day average
Sells
when a holding drops out of the top 30 or below its 200-day average
Trades
US stocks and ETFs

Rules every bot follows

  • Every trade has a stop-loss, and is sized so hitting the stop costs about 1% of that bot's money.
  • No single holding is bigger than 10% of the bot's money, and a bot holds at most 10 at once.
  • A bot stops buying for the day after a 3% daily loss, and pauses after falling 15% from its peak.
  • No borrowing, no short selling, and no leveraged funds.
  • Only liquid stocks and ETFs: over $5 a share and over $20 million traded a day.
  • Only one bot can own a given stock at a time. Who gets first pick rotates daily.

How it runs

A small free cloud server runs the bots once a day after the US market closes, so stock orders fill at the next day's open. Crypto trades every day, including weekends. The server updates this page every 30 minutes with real prices from the Alpaca paper trading account.

A few weeks or months of paper results prove very little, so treat any ranking here as early. Nothing on this page is investment advice.
